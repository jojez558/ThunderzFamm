PRIVACY POLICY.

How Thunderz Family handles booking, account, and purchase information.

Effective date: September 8, 2026

Thunderz Family collects the information you submit through booking forms and checkout, including your name, email, event details, shipping address, and order information. We use it to respond to inquiries, process orders, provide support, and send transactional updates.

Payments are processed by Paystack. We do not store complete payment card numbers. We share information only with service providers needed to operate the site, comply with law, or deliver your order.

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us through the booking page. We retain records only as long as reasonably necessary for business, tax, security, and legal purposes.

Contact Thunderz Family about privacy questions.
