The story behind the movement

BUILT IN RHYTHM.

Thunderz Family is a Nairobi-based dance collective turning street energy, discipline, and African rhythm into unforgettable live experiences.

Our point of view

One crew.
Many languages.

We train as individuals and perform as one. Our work moves between Afro-fusion, Hip-Hop, and Contemporary, finding the line where precision meets instinct.

From warehouse sessions to major stages, every set is made to feel immediate: close, physical, and fully alive. We believe dance can hold a room, open a conversation, and make strangers move together.
